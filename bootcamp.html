Bootcamp

AI-Native Workflow Bootcamp

Build practical AI workflows for real business tasks.

This is not a generic AI tools workshop.

The bootcamp helps teams learn how to identify, redesign, and evaluate the workflows where AI can create real organizational value. It is the practical entry point for teams that want to move from AI experiments to redesigned workflows.

Book a Discovery Call

What participants learn

Teams leave with a method, not just inspiration.

01

Map a real workflow

Participants start with actual business work rather than abstract AI use cases.

02

Identify where AI can help

Teams isolate the steps where AI can improve speed, quality, consistency, or reuse.

03

Redesign the workflow

They redesign the workflow around AI, tools, handoffs, and human judgment instead of dropping AI into an unchanged process.

04

Add human-in-the-loop checkpoints

Participants define where review, escalation, and decision rights should remain visible.

05

Evaluate whether the workflow is better

Teams assess whether the redesign improves speed, quality, consistency, or reuse in a meaningful way.

What makes it different

The bootcamp is not AI training in the generic sense. It gives teams a shared language for workflow redesign and a structured way to find practical AI-native workflows in the work they already do.

Delivered at

Bootcamps and related sessions have reached business leaders, enterprise teams, universities, and professional communities.

Formats

Built for your context and available time.

The core method stays the same across formats. The difference is how much time the group has for workflow mapping, redesign, and discussion.

60 minutes

Lunch and Learn

A compact session for introducing the T-W-O lens and showing why workflow redesign matters.

  • Shared framing on AI-native workflows
  • Live workflow redesign exercise
  • Best for executive teams, internal learning sessions, and community groups

3 hours

Half-Day Bootcamp

The standard working session for teams that want hands-on practice with real business tasks.

  • Workflow mapping and redesign in small groups
  • Human judgment and checkpoint design
  • Participant workflow submissions for follow-on analysis

6 hours

Full-Day Intensive

A deeper session for teams ready to work through a more complex workflow and leave with stronger candidate pilots.

  • Extended redesign of one or more live workflows
  • More time for evaluation criteria and implementation discussion
  • Best for cross-functional teams doing serious workflow redesign

After the Bootcamp

Who it is for

Teams responsible for how work changes, not just what tools they buy.

The bootcamp is designed for business leaders, functional teams, and cross-functional groups working on operations, marketing, service, knowledge work, or internal process redesign. Sessions focus on real business tasks and the workflows around them, not technical implementation details.
