What happens in the room

Three rounds. One realization.

Participants work through a real business scenario: generating, redesigning, and mapping, in structured team exercises.

01

Generate

Teams produce a deliverable using AI, following whatever process feels natural. The process is often chaotic. The outputs are inconsistent. AI can fail because nobody designed the workflow.

02

Redesign

Teams map a structured workflow. By defining handoffs and roles, they eliminate ad-hoc guesswork and ensure repeatable results.

03

Diagnose

Teams map and redesign their own workflows using AI, leaving with a documented workflow ready to run.

The core insight

Roles force handoffs. Handoffs make workflow design visible. The moment teams assign a step to a role (human, AI, or joint), hidden assumptions surface. That is where the redesign begins.
