Workshops

AI changes what your team can do. Workflows need a redesign to match.

Most teams add AI to an unchanged process and call it transformation.

These workshops make the gap visible — the steps nobody owns, the handoffs nobody designed, the judgment calls nobody documented. Then they give teams a method to close it. No technical background required.

What happens in the room

Three rounds. One realization.

Participants work through a real business scenario — generating, diagnosing, and redesigning — in structured team exercises. No slides until the end.

01

Generate

Teams produce a deliverable using AI, following whatever process feels natural. The outputs are inconsistent and the errors are systematic — not because AI failed, but because nobody designed the process.

02

Diagnose

Teams map who owns each step. Disagreement surfaces — especially at the edges between human and AI responsibility. That disagreement is the point. It's the first time most teams have ever had this conversation.

03

Redesign

Teams write the context their workflow actually depends on: the standards, judgment calls, and escalation rules that exist only in people's heads. Most teams have never written this down. Most leave with a first draft.

The core insight

Roles force handoffs. Handoffs make workflow design visible. The moment teams assign a step to a role — human, AI, or joint — they discover assumptions they've never made explicit.

That surface is where the redesign begins.

Formats

Built for your context and time.

Every format runs the same core exercise. Longer sessions add rounds for workflow specification, agent skill design, and building a diagnostic playbook teams keep after the day is done.

60 minutes

Conference Keynote

The audience co-creates a workflow in real time — assigning human and AI roles, stress-testing the design, and arriving at the T-W-O framework together. Works for any executive audience.

3 hours

Half-Day Workshop

Teams run through the full three-round arc in small groups. Suited for MBA cohorts, cross-functional leadership teams, and professional development sessions.

6 hours

Full-Day Intensive

Adds rounds on workflow specification, agent skill design, and building a diagnostic playbook. Participants leave with a documented redesign of a workflow from their own organization.

Who it's for

Leaders responsible for how AI changes work.

Workshops are for business leaders, MBA and EMBA students, and team managers overseeing AI adoption. The exercises run through business scenarios — quarterly reporting, client deliverables, operations workflows — not code or technical infrastructure.

Sessions are available in English and Mandarin, and can be tailored to finance, operations, marketing, or cross-functional leadership audiences.
