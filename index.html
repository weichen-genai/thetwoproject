    How T-W-O helps

    Workflow redesign has to reach the organization layer.

    What is The T-W-O Project?

    The T-W-O Project is a thought-leadership and applied workflow design initiative for organizations adopting generative AI. It connects The Owner, the T-W-O framework, bootcamps, opportunity reports, and AI-Native Workflow Sprints so teams can move from AI experiments to redesigned workflows.

    How does T-W-O help with workflow redesign?

    T-W-O starts with a real workflow, such as claims intake, customer escalation, policy review, or monthly reporting. The work maps current steps, identifies where AI can help, redesigns handoffs and human review, and turns the strongest opportunity into a sprint-ready prototype.

    What changes at the organization level?

    At the organization level, T-W-O clarifies who owns the redesigned workflow, where decision rights sit, what must be governed, and how the team will evaluate whether the new workflow is better than the old one.

    What should a team do first?

    A team should begin with one real workflow that is frequent enough to matter and bounded enough to redesign. The bootcamp builds shared language, the opportunity report helps choose the strongest workflow, and the sprint turns one candidate into a practical prototype.
