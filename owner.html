About

A business novel about who owns the redesign

The Owner follows Ethan, a transformation lead at Northstar Consulting, and two clients who make opposite choices as GenAI pilots collide with workflows, incentives, and the question of who actually redesigns the work once tasks start to move.

Now available in Kindle, paperback, and audiobook, it is the narrative companion to the T-W-O framework for leaders, operators, and builders who care why demos succeed but production change stalls.

The Owner is the narrative companion to the AI-Native Workflow Bootcamp. The book explains why AI pilots stall and why ownership matters. The bootcamp helps teams apply those ideas by redesigning the workflows where transformation actually happens.

Complete book: 48 chapters in three parts. Free public preview: 8 chapters in text and audio.

Part I (1–16)

Speed without workflow change—and the limits of that pattern.

Part II (17–32)

Building the ownership model—and discovering what the organization resists.

Part III (33–48)

The model meets the market—and the question of whether it can outlast the people who built it.
