The T-W-O Project

We study the organizational side of generative AI: how work changes, who owns the change, and how teams learn to make it practical.

What We're Doing

Making the hard parts of GenAI adoption easier to see, discuss, and test.

We use one method in everything we do, from a workshop to a hands-on build: align on how the work happens today, prioritize where AI actually helps, and build a working redesign together.

Align

Get the team looking at the same workflow, the same way.

Prioritize

Decide where AI is worth using, and where it isn't.

Build

Turn the redesign into something real and testable.
