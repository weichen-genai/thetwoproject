About The T-W-O Project

The T-W-O Project helps teams redesign work for the GenAI era by connecting ideas, stories, research, and practical workflow programs.

What It Is

A thought-leadership and applied workflow design initiative.

The T-W-O Project is a thought-leadership and applied workflow design initiative focused on how generative AI changes tasks, workflows, and organizations.

The project is built around a simple idea: AI changes tasks, but transformation happens when teams redesign workflows and organizations around new capabilities.

What T-W-O Means

Three layers of change.

Task

What AI changes at the activity level.

Workflow

How teams redesign the sequence of work around AI, tools, and human judgment.

Organization

How roles, ownership, governance, and coordination evolve around new capabilities.

How The Pieces Fit

The project connects narrative, framework, education, and applied redesign work.

Who Is Behind It

Led by Wei Chen.

The T-W-O Project is led by Wei Chen, Associate Professor of Operations and Information Management at the University of Connecticut School of Business and Academic Director of the Digital Frontiers Initiative.

Wei's research focuses on the business applications, design, and governance of generative AI, including platforms, crowds, fintech, and organizational transformation. His work has appeared in leading journals including Management Science, Information Systems Research, MIS Quarterly, and Production and Operations Management.

Through The T-W-O Project, Wei connects academic research, business storytelling, and practical workflow redesign for organizations adopting generative AI.

Why trust the project

The T-W-O Project connects published research, teaching, executive education, narrative work, and real workflow engagements. It is designed to help organizations think clearly and act practically.

Mission

Closing the gap between AI capability and organizational redesign.

The mission of The T-W-O Project is to close the gap between what AI can do at the task level and what organizations need to redesign in order to benefit from it.

We help teams move from AI experiments to AI-native workflows that combine human judgment, AI capabilities, tools, and evaluation.
