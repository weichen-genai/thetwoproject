What services does The T-W-O Project offer?

The T-W-O Project offers AI-Native Workflow Bootcamps, Workflow Opportunity Reports, and AI-Native Workflow Sprints for teams that want to move beyond AI experiments.

How do teams choose the first workflow to redesign?

Teams start with real workflow submissions and evaluate them by business value, AI feasibility, risk, frequency, and reusability. The best first workflow is important enough to matter and bounded enough to redesign, test, and learn from.

What is a Workflow Opportunity Report?

A Workflow Opportunity Report reviews participant-submitted workflows, scores them on business value, AI feasibility, risk, frequency, and reusability, then identifies top candidates and next-step pilots.

What is an AI-Native Workflow Sprint?

An AI-Native Workflow Sprint is a 2-4 week engagement to select one workflow, map the current process, redesign it with AI, tools, and human judgment, and build a lightweight prototype or demo.
