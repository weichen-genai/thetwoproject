Work with Us

A practical path for teams that want to move beyond AI experiments.

The T-W-O Project stays grounded in a serious idea about work. These engagements turn that idea into a method for identifying, redesigning, and prototyping AI-native workflows.

1

For teams that need a shared language and method

AI-Native Workflow Bootcamp

What happens

  • Interactive session
  • Hands-on workflow redesign exercises
  • Collection of real workflow submissions
  • Team discussion and reflection

What you leave with

  • Shared understanding of AI-native workflows
  • Initial workflow ideas
  • Candidate workflows for deeper analysis

2

For leaders who want to know what to redesign first

Workflow Opportunity Report

What happens

  • Review participant-submitted workflows
  • Score workflows on business value, AI feasibility, risk, frequency, and reusability
  • Identify top opportunities
  • Recommend next-step pilots

What you leave with

  • Executive-ready opportunity map
  • Top workflow candidates
  • Recommended pilot roadmap

3

For teams ready to prototype what works

AI-Native Workflow Sprint

What happens

  • Select one workflow
  • Map the current process
  • Redesign it with AI, tools, and human judgment
  • Build a lightweight prototype or demo
  • Define evaluation criteria and implementation roadmap

What you leave with

  • Redesigned AI-native workflow
  • Prototype or demo
  • Human-in-the-loop design
  • Evaluation rubric
  • Implementation plan
