What we provide

Three services, one workflow redesign method.

The work can start as a shared learning session, a prioritization report, or a focused sprint. The common thread is the same: choose a live workflow, redesign the sequence, and make ownership explicit.

1. Align

AI-Native Workflow Bootcamp

A working session that gives teams a shared language for mapping work, spotting AI opportunities, and collecting real workflow candidates.

  • Hands-on workflow mapping
  • Team discussion and examples
  • Candidate workflows for follow-up

2. Prioritize

Workflow Opportunity Report

A review of submitted workflows that ranks where AI-native redesign is most likely to create value without creating avoidable risk.

  • Opportunity scoring
  • Risk and feasibility review
  • Recommended pilot roadmap

3. Build

AI-Native Workflow Sprint

A focused 2-4 week engagement to redesign one high-value workflow and turn the new operating sequence into a prototype or demo.

  • Workflow redesign and prototype
  • Human-in-the-loop checkpoints
  • Evaluation criteria and implementation plan

Bootcamp formats

Built for your context and available time.

The core bootcamp method stays the same across formats. The difference is how much time the group has for workflow mapping, redesign, and discussion.

60 minutes

Lunch and Learn

A compact session for introducing the T-W-O lens and showing why workflow redesign matters.

  • Shared framing on AI-native workflows
  • Live workflow redesign exercise
  • Best for executive teams, internal learning sessions, and community groups

3 hours

Half-Day Bootcamp

The standard working session for teams that want hands-on practice with real business tasks.

  • Workflow mapping and redesign in small groups
  • Human judgment and checkpoint design
  • Participant workflow submissions for follow-on analysis

6 hours

Full-Day Intensive

A deeper session for teams ready to work through a more complex workflow and leave with stronger candidate pilots.

  • Extended redesign of one or more live workflows
  • More time for evaluation criteria and implementation discussion
  • Best for cross-functional teams doing serious workflow redesign
